    How this website works

    Follow these steps to keep track of your parcels and plan for delivery.

    1

    Add a tracking ID

    Paste the courier tracking ID and choose the courier if the website cannot identify it automatically.

    2

    Save multiple parcels

    Add tracking IDs from different shopping platforms. If you connect your email with permission, the website can match order emails and show product pictures automatically.

    3

    Check delivery updates

    See courier status updates and estimated arrival windows when that tracking information is available.

    4

    Plan the handoff

    Set delivery preferences or a backup recipient when the courier offers those options.

    5

    Get help if something goes wrong

    Report a missed delivery or another parcel issue, or contact Customer Care.
